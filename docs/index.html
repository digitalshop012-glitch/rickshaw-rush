Privacy Policy for Rickshaw Rush

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Rickshaw Rush ("the app", "we") is a free casual game. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app stores your best score on your own device only. This data never leaves your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the device's advertising ID, IP address, and general device and usage information to show ads and measure their performance. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more: Google Advertising Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Ad data is processed by Google as described above.
